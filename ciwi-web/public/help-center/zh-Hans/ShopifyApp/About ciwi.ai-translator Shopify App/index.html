About ciwi.ai-translator Shopify App

ciwi.ai is dedicated to leveraging AI large language models to help eCommerce merchants enhance global reach and boost conversion rates.
ciwi.ai-translator is our intelligent translation and localization tool designed for Shopify merchants. Powered by advanced GPT technology, it supports over 50 languages and delivers high-precision, customizable, SEO-friendly translations — enabling your store to enter global markets with ease.

Whether you are a cross-border brand, an independent store owner, or a startup looking to expand overseas, ciwi.ai-translator helps you manage and optimize multilingual content at lower cost and higher efficiency.

✨ Why Choose ciwi.ai-translator?​

  • High-Precision AI Translations
    Powered by the latest GPT technology, delivering more natural results and closer alignment with your brand voice than Shopify’s built-in translation.

  • Deep Customization
    Combine manual edits with AI translations to maintain your preferred tone and style.

  • Efficient & Convenient
    Manage translations directly from the Shopify dashboard without complex import/export steps.

  • Full Compatibility
    Works seamlessly with Shopify themes, products, collections, pages, and more — all while keeping SEO intact.

📚 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)​

Q: What content of my Shopify store can I translate?
A: You can translate all translatable resources in your store, including products, collections, themes, pages, and more.

Q: How much does ciwi.ai-translator cost?
A: Installation is free. You only pay when you use our AI model for translations.
Translating 1,000 words consumes around 1,000–3,000 credits (about $0.5–$1.5 per 1,000 words).
New users receive 200,000 free credits.
We also offer subscription plans that provide more credits at a lower cost along with additional premium features.

Q: How can I contact the support team?
A: Email us at support@ciwi.ai and we will respond as soon as possible.

Q: What happens if I uninstall ciwi.ai-translator?
A: Your AI translations will remain in your store as your own assets.

Q: Can I request new features?
A: Absolutely! Send your suggestions to support@ciwi.ai — we review every request carefully.
