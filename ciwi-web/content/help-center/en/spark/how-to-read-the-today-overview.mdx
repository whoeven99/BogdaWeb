The Today view gives you a daily read of the store without stitching together multiple reports.

## Metrics to watch

The Today view surfaces the core signals:

- **Revenue** and **profit**
- **Conversion**
- **Traffic**
- **ROI**
- **Orders**
- **Cost** and **customer value**

Use these to see what changed today and where to look next.

## Diagnosis report

The diagnosis report reviews core metrics and store health over a recent window, such as the last seven days. It highlights areas that need attention instead of just showing charts.

## Drill into a signal

When a metric moves, open it to see the supporting breakdown and ask Spark for an explanation and next steps. From there you can turn the insight into a task.
