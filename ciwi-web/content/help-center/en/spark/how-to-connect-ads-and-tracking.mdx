Spark connects to your ad and analytics accounts so tracking and catalog data stay aligned with the store.

## Connect your accounts

From the ads or settings area, connect the platforms you use:

- **Meta** — ads, catalog, and pixel.
- **Google** — ads, Analytics, Search Console, and Merchant Center.
- **TikTok** — ads, catalog, and pixel.

Follow the OAuth flow for each platform and grant the requested permissions.

## Set up pixels and tracking

For Meta and TikTok, configure the pixel and verify storefront tracking. Spark can help validate that events fire correctly after setup.

## Sync catalogs

Sync your Shopify products to Meta, Google, or TikTok catalogs. Review the sync status and fix any validation errors before running ads.

## Check connection health

Spark surfaces link status and feed issues so you can see which channels are connected and whether anything needs attention.
