Spark is an AI store assistant embedded in Shopify. It connects store data, daily metrics, ads and tracking, content workflows, and operations tasks into one workspace.

## What Spark covers

- **AI assistant** — ask questions about store performance and run tasks through chat.
- **Today overview** — read revenue, profit, conversion, traffic, ROI, orders, cost, and more in one view.
- **Diagnosis report** — review core metrics and store health over a recent window.
- **Ads and tracking** — connect Meta, Google, and TikTok, set up pixels, and sync catalogs.
- **Product improve** — generate and refine product descriptions and score product quality.
- **Image tools** — generate images and translate text inside images.
- **PageSpeed** — audit storefront performance.
- **Tasks** — manage AI and automation tasks from one list.

## How it fits together

Spark is built around a chat workspace. You ask about a signal, Spark explains what may be happening and why it matters, and you can turn that into a task or a content change without leaving the app.
