According to the current **Pricing** page policy in the app:

## Refunds

Ciwi does **not** offer refunds for plan charges or credit purchases. You can cancel future billing, but the current period continues until the billing window ends.

## Subscription credits

Subscription-plan credits reset at the end of each billing cycle. They are meant to support the active subscription period rather than accumulate forever.

## Extra purchased credits

Extra credits are treated separately from plan credits. The current Pricing copy states that extra credits do not expire and are used when plan credits run out.

## Cancel or downgrade behavior

The current Pricing policy also states that unused credits remain available for **3 more months** after cancellation or downgrade, even though lower plans may expose fewer features.

Because billing behavior is product policy, always check the latest wording on the in-app Pricing page if you are making a purchase decision.
