Ciwi currently covers a broad Shopify translation surface. You can choose the modules you want in **Translate v4** instead of translating the whole store blindly.

## Main content modules

- products and product options
- collections
- blogs and articles
- pages
- navigation and links
- filters

## Structured and store-level content

- shop information
- policies
- metafields
- metaobjects
- selling plans and related store data

## Theme and system content

- theme templates
- theme section groups
- theme settings content
- locale content inside the theme
- email templates
- delivery methods
- shipping or packing-slip templates

## Optional custom Liquid content

Ciwi can also include pending custom or third-party storefront text through the custom Liquid pipeline when you enable it in the job setup.

## Important note

Not every module should be treated the same way. Core merchandising content often benefits from deeper review, while lower-risk modules can move faster once your glossary and workflow are stable.
