The fastest way to check your current balance is to open the **Pricing** page in the app.

## What you should look at

- available credits
- whether launch or trial credits are included
- the estimate shown before your next translation task

## Why the estimate matters

A balance number alone does not tell you whether your next job is affordable. Always compare the available credits with the task estimate in **Translate v4**, especially when you select multiple target languages or more content modules.

## Helpful rule

Check credits **before** you launch a large job, not after it fails a quota gate. That keeps the workflow smoother and makes budget planning easier.
