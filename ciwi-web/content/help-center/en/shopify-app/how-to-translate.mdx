The current Ciwi translation workflow is built around four product areas: **Language**, **Translate v4**, **Manage Translation**, and **Publish**.

## Step 1: Add the target language

Open the **Language** page and add the market language you want to support. This defines which targets Ciwi can use in later translation jobs.

## Step 2: Prepare quality controls

Before a large run, add glossary entries for your most important brand and product terms. This is the easiest way to reduce repeated wording issues across pages and products.

## Step 3: Create jobs in Translate v4

Open **Translate v4** and choose:

- one or more target languages
- the Shopify content modules you want to translate
- the AI model
- whether to overwrite existing translations
- whether to include custom Liquid content when needed

The app shows an estimate before you commit. If credits are not enough, you can buy more and continue.

## Step 4: Monitor job progress

Each selected target language becomes its own job. That makes it easier to track progress, retry one language, or review results market by market.

## Step 5: Review in Manage Translation

After the job completes, use **Manage Translation** to inspect modules such as products, collections, pages, navigation, theme content, metafields, and more. This is the right place to polish important copy before storefront release.

## Step 6: Publish the language

Go back to the **Language** page and publish the target language when you are ready. Then preview the storefront and check the pages that matter most for conversion.
