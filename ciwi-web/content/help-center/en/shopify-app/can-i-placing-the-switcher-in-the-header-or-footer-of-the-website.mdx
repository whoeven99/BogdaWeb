Yes. In practice, merchants usually place the Ciwi switcher in a header, footer, or another clearly visible theme section.

## Header vs footer

- **Header** is better when language selection is a primary shopper action.
- **Footer** works well when you want the switcher available without competing with top navigation.

## What matters more than the exact section

- Shoppers should be able to find it quickly.
- It should stay usable on mobile.
- It should not conflict with the cart, account menu, or sticky navigation.

## Styling tips

The current switcher settings support control over visibility details such as selectors, colors, flags, transparency, and position-related settings. Start with clarity first, then style it to fit the theme.

## Recommendation

If you are still deciding, start in a highly visible but non-intrusive place, test mobile behavior, and confirm that both language and currency switching are easy to understand.
