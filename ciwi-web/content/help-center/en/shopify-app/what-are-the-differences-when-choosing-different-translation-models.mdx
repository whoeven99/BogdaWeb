Compare AI translation models using the tradeoff that matters for your content: wording quality, speed, or cost. **Translate v4** offers multiple model options, so test a representative sample before translating a larger scope.

## A practical way to choose

- Choose a faster, lower-cost model when you want broad coverage quickly.
- Choose a stronger model when copy quality, context handling, and nuance matter more.
- Test on real storefront content instead of judging only from a generic sentence sample.

## What changes when the model changes

- translation speed
- credit usage
- tone and fluency
- how well the model handles dense product copy or marketing language

## What does not change

Model choice does not replace glossary, review, or publishing discipline. Those still matter for stable quality, especially on high-value pages.

## Recommendation

Start with one priority market, compare output on a few representative products or pages, and then standardize on the model that gives the best balance for your store.
