Use the **Currency** page to prepare the monetary side of localization before you rely on storefront switching.

## What you can configure

- add supported currencies
- edit exchange rates
- choose how price rounding behaves
- review how the storefront will display price changes

## Recommended setup order

1. Add the currencies you actually need.
2. Decide whether automatic or manual exchange rates fit your business.
3. Configure rounding so the displayed prices match your pricing strategy.
4. Test the storefront before you turn on geolocation-based currency behavior.

## Why preview matters

Currency setup is not just a data exercise. You should check how prices appear on product pages, collection cards, cart surfaces, and mobile layouts so the localization feels trustworthy to shoppers.

## Connection with the switcher

Multi-currency works best when the storefront switcher is already visible and shoppers can understand or override the selected currency themselves.
