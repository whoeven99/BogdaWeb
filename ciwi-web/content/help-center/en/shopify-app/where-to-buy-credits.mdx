You can buy more credits inside the app.

## Where merchants usually start the purchase flow

- from the **Pricing** page
- from the quota gate that appears when a new translation task is larger than the current balance

## What to do before paying

1. check the estimate for the upcoming task
2. confirm whether you need extra credits only for this run or for a broader launch plan
3. choose the pack or plan option that matches that workload

## After payment

You can return to the translation flow and continue. Extra credits help you keep running credit-based actions, but they do not replace the need to review important content before publishing.
