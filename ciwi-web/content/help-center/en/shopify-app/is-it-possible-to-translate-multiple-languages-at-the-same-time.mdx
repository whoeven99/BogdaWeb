Yes. In the current **Translate v4** flow, you can select multiple target languages in one setup and create jobs for all of them together.

## How Ciwi handles it

Ciwi does **not** merge all selected languages into one single opaque task. Instead, it creates **one job per target language**.

That means you can:

- track progress per market
- retry one language without rerunning everything
- understand failures more clearly
- review output market by market

## Why this is better

Different markets often need different review intensity, launch timing, or storefront checks. Separate jobs keep that operationally manageable.

## Recommendation

Even though the app supports multi-target creation, it is still smart to start with your top-priority markets first, validate wording and storefront quality, and then expand further.
