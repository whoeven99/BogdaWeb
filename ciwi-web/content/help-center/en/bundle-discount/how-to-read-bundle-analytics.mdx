The Analytics page shows how your bundle offers are performing. Use it to compare offers and time windows, then decide which campaigns to keep or adjust.

## Filters

- **Time range** — choose the last 7, 30, or 60 days.
- **Offer** — view all bundle deals together or focus on a single offer.

## Metrics

- **Visitors** — the number of visitors exposed to the offer.
- **Bundle orders** — the number of orders that used a bundle.
- **Conversion to bundle** — the share of visitors that became bundle orders.

## Daily added revenue

The line chart shows the additional revenue the app generated each day. Use it to spot trends and compare how an offer performs over time.
