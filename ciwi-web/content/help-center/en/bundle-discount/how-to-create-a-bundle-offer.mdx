Creating an offer follows one builder flow: pick the campaign type, compose the products and rules, then set schedule and targeting before publishing.

## 1. Start a new offer

From **All offers**, open **Create offer**. Choose the offer type or a starter template that matches what you want to sell: quantity breaks, BXGY, or complete bundle. The template only sets your starting module; you can add more modules later.

## 2. Compose the offer

Add the trigger product and the bundle item pool. For a complete bundle, choose how many items the shopper can select and set the bundle pricing mode:

- `Full price`
- `Percentage off`
- `Amount off`
- `Fixed price`

For quantity breaks and BXGY, set the buy and get quantities and the discount value.

## 3. Set the discount rules

Review the discount preview on the right. Product discount modules compete with each other, and the discount that saves the shopper the most wins on overlapping cart lines. Free gift and other order rewards stack separately.

## 4. Schedule and target

Choose which products, markets, and customers the offer applies to, and set a schedule if the campaign is time-limited.

## 5. Publish

Save and publish the offer. The app compiles the campaign into storefront and Shopify Function payloads. Check the live store to confirm the offer blocks render as expected.
